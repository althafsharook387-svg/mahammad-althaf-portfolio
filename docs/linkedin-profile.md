**4. MBA Research — IPO Fund Allocation & Equity Prices** · 2025 · Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management
```
An 89-page dissertation on 330 mainboard IPOs listed on the NSE and BSE through 2024,
testing whether OFS-heavy issues perform differently from expansion-focused ones.
Method: CAPM, alpha–beta, independent-sample t-tests, regression, correlation and a
decision tree. Data from RHP prospectuses, SEBI filings, Chittorgarh, Moneycontrol,
Screener and Yahoo Finance; built in Advanced Excel, Python, SPSS and Power BI.

Findings — expansion-focused vs. OFS-heavy: average subscription 214.59× vs 53.68×,
listing-day return 52.41% vs 27.47%, alpha 43.21% vs 18.70%, beta 0.418 vs 0.328.
The more an issue is weighted toward expansion capital, the stronger it lists — with
more market sensitivity attached. Grey market premium predicted almost nothing
(GMP ↔ listing-day return: +0.081).

Skills: Financial Analysis · CAPM · Regression Analysis · SPSS · Python · Power BI
```
