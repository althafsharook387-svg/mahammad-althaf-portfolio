About

Operations on one side, data on the other.

I'm an MBA in Finance & Marketing working in procurement and import operations. Day to day that means coordinating international suppliers, freight forwarders, logistics partners and banks — and making sure the paperwork behind each shipment holds up.

That covers Letters of Credit end to end, import documentation, and EPCG and Advance Authorisation processes with their export obligations tracked to closure. It's detailed work where a missed date has a real cost.

The other half is analysis. I build Power BI dashboards and MIS reports on procurement performance, supplier benchmarking and cost, and I use AI tools to move faster through research, drafting and interpretation. Operations generates the data; analytics is what makes it useful.

MBA research

Impact of IPO Fund Allocation on Equity Prices

Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management · Visvesvaraya Technological University · guided by Asst. Prof. Ramesh K G

330IPOs analysed
2Exchanges · NSE & BSE
8Week study
89Page report

01 Research question

When an Indian company lists, proceeds either go to existing shareholders cashing out (Offer for Sale) or into the business itself (expansion-focused fresh issue). Does that choice change listing performance and risk?

02 Method

CAPM for expected versus actual return, alpha–beta for excess performance, independent-sample t-tests for significance, a decision tree to rank drivers, plus regression and correlation.

03 Data

330 mainboard IPOs listed on the NSE and BSE through 2024, from RHP prospectuses, SEBI filings, Chittorgarh, Moneycontrol, Screener and Yahoo Finance.

04 Tools

Advanced Excel for the dataset, Python and SPSS for statistical testing, Power BI for visualisation and the dashboard narrative.

Conclusion

The higher the OFS proportion in an issue, the weaker its listing performance — and the more an issue is weighted toward expansion capital, the stronger it performs. That upside carries more market sensitivity, so it reads as a risk–return tradeoff rather than a free lunch. Grey market hype predicted almost nothing.
